Prototype note: the leaf is first separated from the background, then only uneven spots on the leaf are measured. This is computed live in your browser, on-device, with no internet needed. The disease label comes from a rule-based stand-in that will be replaced by the trained MobileNetV3 (TensorFlow Lite) model. This is decision support, not a substitute for an agriculture officer.
